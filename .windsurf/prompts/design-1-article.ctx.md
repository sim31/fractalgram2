Respect Game game seems to be a core component of fractal organizations as we know them. The fractally whitepaper [^1] which inspired most of the ecosystem included a lot of other features you would want in a DAO, plus we have considered many more, but respect game remains as the one constant across time and different fractals, that distinguish them from other DAO initiatives. This highlights the importance of an app / tool we use for playing respect game.

We have experimented with multiple apps so far, but I think none of them "get" the respect game completely right, yet. Also different implementations of the process appeared some of which deviate from some of the original principles quite a lot.

## Function of Respect Game in Fractals
The distinguishing feature of fractals is that governance token is distributed using a social consensus game. While a lot of the crypto world is trying ways to codify and automatize such things like impact measurement, public goods funding and token distribution, fractals leaving it to the people (their collective consensus) to decide (but still in a scalable way). Yes, this implies that there's subjectivity involved in the process and while a lot of people would be quick to judge that as a weakness, it is actually a strength.

Not everything has an objective metric to measure things by. And even if there are metrics to measure certain things, you then bump into Gall's Law: "When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure". Respect game does not have these problems. A group of people playing respect game can consider a much wider range of signals, most of which are not and often cannot be onchain. They can also adapt to different situations meeting by meeting. Public good funding platforms, which try to measure things objectively, cannot compete with this level of awareness, flexibility and adaptability.

But how much we make use of this strength depends on how we play Respect Game. If we just vote on a ranking and do not discuss our votes, we miss a lot of the benefits of Respect Game. Maybe one of the participants sees something important about the value of specific contribution that others miss. If there's no discussion, this information is not shared and the result will not take into account that datapoint. But if people discuss their rationale for their votes, you get the actual benefit of 3-5 people reviewing a single contribution. Again - awareness to different pieces of information and different perspectives.

Furthermore if this sharing of perspective leads to actual discussion where people move from disagreement to mutual agreement, there's a great chance that accuracy and fairness of the result will be improved. This is the dialectic principle - by being able to present and consider different viewpoints we are able to arrive at a viewpoint that is superior to all others.

**Function of Respect Game in an organization is to involve collective human awareness and intelligence in distribution of reputation token.**
